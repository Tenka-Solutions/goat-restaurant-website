# Client content checklist

## Required before launch

- [ ] Definitive domain; replace `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` and regenerate the QR.
- [ ] Contact-form recipient email.
- [ ] Resend API key and a verified sender/domain for `CONTACT_FROM_EMAIL`.
- [ ] Restaurant address, map URL, phone and public email.
- [ ] Opening hours, holiday-hours policy and timezone.
- [ ] Approved Instagram, Facebook and TikTok URLs.
- [ ] External reservation URL, or approval to send the navbar CTA to contact.
- [ ] Review every question in `docs/menu-content-review.md`.
- [ ] Approve English menu translations and normalized spelling.
- [ ] Confirm currency, taxes, price validity and availability wording.
- [ ] Supply the real bakery catalogue (products, descriptions, prices and availability), or approve the coming-soon state.

## CMS and promotions

- [ ] Create/confirm the Sanity project ID and dataset.
- [ ] Invite named editors and decide their Sanity roles based on the purchased plan.
- [ ] Supply promotion/event images with usage rights and bilingual alt text.
- [ ] Supply bilingual titles, descriptions, CTA labels, links, dates, times and timezone.
- [ ] Confirm whether any event has enough verified information for Event structured data.

## Brand, legal and operational

- [ ] Confirm that current generated photographs may be published or replace them with approved assets.
- [ ] Approve all institutional copy and the editorial quote.
- [ ] Confirm privacy/consent wording and any required privacy-policy link for the operating jurisdiction.
- [ ] Confirm business legal name if it must appear in the footer.
- [ ] Scan and test the production QR on iOS and Android before printing.
